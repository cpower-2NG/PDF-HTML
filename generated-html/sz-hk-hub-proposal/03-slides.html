双城生活的现实挑战Cross-Border Life: Fragmented Info, Poor Real-Time Data, Compliance Risks

  • 政策、口岸、金融服务更新频繁，用户难以持续跟踪。Policies, port services, and financial rules change fast — hard to track.
  • 实时数据（汇率、人流、班次）缺失导致决策失误。Missing real-time data (FX rates, crowd, schedules) leads to bad decisions.
  • 跨境金融合规边界模糊，通用 LLM 容易产生幻觉。Cross-border compliance rules are vague; general LLMs hallucinate.
  • 跨域信息分散在政府、银行、交通与活动平台。Info scattered across gov, banks, transit, and event platforms.
